# Pro: Static and Dynamic Progression

[Back to lesson plan index](README.md)

The supplied progression chart separates the Pro progression into Static and
Dynamic tracks. Eagle content below is summarized from the September 2024 IBA
Static Progression Coach Manual. Other chart-listed skills are included without
inferred prerequisites.

## Static Progression

Use controlled head-up and head-down transitions to complete paired vertical
maneuvers while preserving level, heading, and separation.

### Half Eagle

**Prerequisite:** Head-Down Transitions.

**Desired outcome:** Two flyers exchange head-up and head-down orientations
through a controlled half Eagle while maintaining a clear path, heading, and
altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #57.

**Key points:**

- The head-down flyer transitions under the head-up flyer.
- The head-up flyer transitions over the head-down flyer.
- Both flyers finish in the opposite orientation.
- Maintain eye contact and a consistent center throughout.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #28.

**Coaching and safety:** Brief both slots, initiation, flight paths, eye
contact, levels, abort, and separation. Demonstrate the component transition
solo before adding the second flyer.

### Full Eagle

**Prerequisite:** Head-Down Transitions.

**Desired outcome:** Two flyers complete a continuous full Eagle and return to
their original orientations with minimal heading or altitude change.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #59.

**Key points:**

- Begin with the same over-under relationship used for the half Eagle.
- Continue through the role exchange instead of stopping halfway.
- Each flyer returns to the starting orientation.
- Maintain eye contact throughout the maneuver.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #28.

**Coaching and safety:** Brief both slots, initiation, flight paths, eye
contact, levels, abort, and separation. Build the full maneuver only after both
flyers can complete each half safely and predictably.
