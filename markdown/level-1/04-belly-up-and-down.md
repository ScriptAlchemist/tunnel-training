## Belly Up & Down

**Prerequisite:** Belly-fly turns plus forward and backward movement.

**Desired outcome:** Move up, stop, and move down in a controlled manner
without moving forward, backward, or changing heading.

**Key points:**

- To move up, flatten the torso and then extend the arms and legs evenly to
  increase surface area.
- Return to the neutral belly position to stop upward movement.
- To move down, increase the arch and reduce surface area by bending the arms
  and legs.
- Balance arm and leg changes to prevent unwanted drive and use small inputs to
  maintain heading.

**Coaching and safety:** This skill travels vertically through the center of
the chamber. Set an upper stopping point, manage height carefully, and begin
with small fall-rate changes before increasing the range.
