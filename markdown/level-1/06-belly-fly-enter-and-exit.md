## Belly Fly Enter & Exit

**Prerequisite:** Belly-fly turns, forward and backward movement, and up and
down movement.

**Desired outcome:** Enter into a neutral belly position and approach and exit
the doorway safely, in control, at an appropriate altitude, and with minimal
instructor assistance.

**Key points:**

- Enter from a stable stance with the chin up and hips low toward the center of
  the tunnel.
- Use arms forward and bent legs to control forward drive, then use small
  upper-body inputs to maintain a straight heading.
- For the exit, face the doorway from roughly five feet above the net and move
  forward slowly.
- Stop at the doorway before taking the frame; bend the knees to stand and step
  out instead of reaching early.

**Coaching and safety:** The tunnel instructor controls permission to enter.
Brief the doorway path and stopping point, avoid uncontrolled vertical travel,
and increase independence only after the student shows consistent control.
