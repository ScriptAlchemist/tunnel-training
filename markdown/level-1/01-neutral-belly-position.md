## Neutral Belly Position

**Prerequisite:** Signed off by an IBA instructor as safe to begin the IBA
Flyer Progression.

**Desired outcome:** Fly at a neutral wind speed with full control in a stable,
neutral belly position and without excessive movement or rotation.

**Key points:**

- Maintain a symmetrical arch with the head raised and legs shoulder-width
  apart.
- In the basic position, keep the arms near 90 degrees with elbows around eye
  level, toes pointed, and knees slightly higher than the hips.
- In the advanced position, press the elbows down, bring the hands closer
  together, and raise the chest without losing symmetry.
- Use a fixed heading and visual reference; avoid facing directly toward or
  away from the doorway.

**Coaching and safety:** Begin at an agreed wind speed and keep the student's
altitude within their ability. Early instruction should emphasize relaxation,
symmetry, and small corrections rather than too many inputs at once.
