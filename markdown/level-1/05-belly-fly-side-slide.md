## Belly Fly Side Slide

**Prerequisite:** Belly-fly up and down movement.

**Desired outcome:** Move sideways in both directions using balanced whole-body
inputs, then stop on heading without gaining or losing altitude.

**Key points:**

- Begin in neutral at the center or one side of the tunnel.
- Lower the leading-side elbow, shoulder, and knee slightly while extending the
  trailing arm.
- Balance each input to preserve heading and apply the opposite input to stop.
- For faster slides, increase whole-body pitch and begin the stopping input
  earlier.

**Coaching and safety:** Define the travel path and stopping point before the
flight. Start with small movements and increase distance or speed only after
the student demonstrates consistent heading and altitude control.
