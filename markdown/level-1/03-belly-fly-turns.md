## Belly Fly Turns

**Prerequisite:** Belly-fly forward and backward movement, on or off the net.

**Desired outcome:** Complete controlled turns in both directions using
balanced upper- and lower-body inputs, beginning and ending on a predetermined
heading.

**Key points:**

- Begin and finish every turn in the neutral position.
- For a basic turn, look slightly toward the turn and lower the shoulder on
  that side while maintaining the arch.
- Apply an opposite input to stop on the selected heading.
- For advanced turns, coordinate the shoulders and knees to rotate around the
  body's center instead of orbiting around the tunnel.

**Coaching and safety:** Give the student a clear heading reference and enough
room to stop. Watch for drift, excessive banking, and loss of altitude during
the turn.
