## Required belly-flying foundation

The flyer must have completed the IBA Level 1 Flyer Skills. Before beginning
back-flying, the flyer should be able to:

- Demonstrate all eight points of motion while belly-flying.
- Fly those movements comfortably.
- Enter and exit the chamber with minimal instructor assistance.

## Required briefing

The instructor must brief the flyer on the differences between belly-flying and
back-flying entrances and exits, along with the safety considerations specific
to back-flying.
