## Required flying foundation

Before beginning head-down flight, the flyer should be competent in:

- All belly-flying and back-flying skills.
- All Level 3 head-up skills.
- At least the head-up-to-head-up front flip transition; proficiency in the
  other head-up transitions is preferred.

## Required briefing

The tunnel instructor and head-down coach must brief the on-net setup, staged
release, selected body position, bail signal and recovery path, spotting plan,
and the local response if the flyer's head passes through the net.

> Source: IBA Static Progression Coach Manual, v2.0, Level 4 Lesson Plan #22
> and Flight Tutorials #42-43, pages 104-110.
