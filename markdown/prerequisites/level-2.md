## Progression requirement

Before beginning the Level 2 back-flying progression, the flyer must:

- Complete the IBA Level 1 Flyer Skills and be signed off as an IBA Flyer Level 1.
- Demonstrate all eight points of motion while belly-flying.
- Fly those movements comfortably and enter and exit the chamber with minimal
  instructor assistance.

## Instructor briefing

Before learning back-flying, receive an instructor briefing covering:

- How back-flying entrances and exits differ from belly-flying entrances and
  exits.
- The safety considerations specific to back-flying.
