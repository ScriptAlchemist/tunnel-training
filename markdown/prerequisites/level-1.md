## Entry requirement

Before starting the Level 1 belly-flying skills, the flyer must be signed off by
an IBA instructor as safe to progress through the IBA Flyer Progression.

> Source: IBA Static Progression Coach Manual, v2.0, Level 1 Belly-Fly
> Lesson Plan #1, page 24.
