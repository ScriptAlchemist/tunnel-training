## Required back-flying foundation

Before beginning head-up flight, the flyer should be able to:

- Maintain a stable back-flying position and control all points of motion at
  the wind speed used for head-up training.
- Walk into and out of the tunnel safely at the required wind speed.
- Recognize instability and recover to a neutral back-flying position.

## Required briefing

The coach and instructor must agree on the wind-speed plan, the on-net starting
position, the head-up signals, and the bail procedure before training begins.
