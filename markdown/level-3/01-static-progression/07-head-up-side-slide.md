### Head Up Side Slide

**Prerequisite:** Head Up Up and Down Movement.

**Desired outcome:** Shift sideways in both directions with balanced upper- and
lower-body inputs while maintaining heading and altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #33.

**Key points:**

- Start in the center or at one side with the path clear of the doorway.
- Lower and bend the leading arm while extending and raising the trailing arm.
- Lower and angle the leading leg while lifting the trailing leg, balancing the
  upper and lower body to prevent a turn.
- Keep the spine straight and apply the opposing side-slide input early enough
  to stop before the wall.

**Coaching and safety:** Establish the direction, references, and stop limit
before starting. Teach the input on or near the net, increase speed gradually,
and watch for excessive pitch or a late stop.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #20.
