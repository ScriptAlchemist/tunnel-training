### Head Up Forward & Backward

**Prerequisite:** Neutral and Stable Head Up Position.

**Desired outcome:** Move forward and backward from neutral head-up flight,
stop between movements, and maintain altitude, stability, and control.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #30.

**Key points:**

- Begin low to the net and use small, gradual movements.
- For basic forward drive, keep the lower body neutral and lean the upper body
  slightly back into the airflow; lift the knees to reduce the drive and stop.
- For backward drive, bring the chest slightly toward the airflow and stop with
  the opposing input.
- For stronger movement, coordinate the torso with controlled heel and leg
  inputs while keeping the body balanced.
- Return to neutral after every stop and recover to neutral back flight if the
  position becomes unstable.

**Coaching and safety:** Define the travel path and stopping points before the
flight. Head-up pitch can create rapid drive, so progress from slow starts and
stops, leave generous wall clearance, and increase speed only after control is
demonstrated.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #17.
