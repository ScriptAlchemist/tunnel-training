## Formations

Add controlled entrances and shared formations after the required individual
flight skills are established.

### 2-Way Entrances

**Prerequisite:** Super Positioning.

**Desired outcome:** Enter as a linked two-flyer group and establish the
briefed formation at a controlled, low altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorials #8, #9, and #10.

**Manual scope:** The Formation manual includes linked group entrances in its
Level 2 two-way lesson. The progression chart displays entrances in Level 3,
so this page keeps that chart placement.

**Key points:**

- Brief the entrance order, grips, slots, level, and release plan before the
  flight.
- Both flyers are responsible for stopping the entrance and remaining low in
  the tunnel.
- Maintain visual contact and establish a stable formation before beginning
  another movement.
- If a flyer becomes unstable, that flyer waits for the instructor while the
  stable flyer moves low and away.

**Coaching and safety:** Coordinate the entire entrance with the on-duty
instructor. Use only a formation and entry method appropriate to both flyers'
demonstrated skill.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #8.

### 3-Way Formations

**Prerequisite:** 2-Way Flying.

**Desired outcome:** Fly with two other people while maintaining control and
visual awareness, then complete predetermined formations and selections from
the three-way dive pool.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #12.

**Key points:**

- Begin with basic formations, small movements, and easy transitions.
- Maintain visual contact and stop in a neutral body position before starting
  the next movement.
- Coordinate vertical exercises with clear timing and visual references.
- Enter into assigned slots and increase complexity only after the group can
  maintain level, heading, and control.

**Coaching and safety:** Use three qualified flyers or an appropriate
flyer-and-coach combination. Brief slots, entrances, transitions, recovery,
and separation; if one flyer becomes unstable, that flyer waits for the
instructor while stable flyers move low and away.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #9.
