# Level 3: Head Up, Dynamic and Formations

[Back to lesson plan index](README.md)

The supplied progression chart separates Level 3 into Static Progression,
Dynamic, and Formation tracks. Static lesson content below is summarized from
the September 2024 IBA Static Progression Coach Manual, and Dynamic lesson
content is summarized from the corresponding Dynamic Progression Coach Manual.
Formation lesson content is summarized from the corresponding Formation
Skydiving Coach Manual while retaining the chart's level arrangement.

## Static Progression

Build a stable head-up position and connect it to controlled movement and
transitions.

### High Speed Control

**Description:** Develop the control needed to fly safely as wind speed increases.

**Source status:** This skill appears in the supplied progression chart, but the
2024 Static Progression Coach Manual does not provide a standalone lesson plan
for it. Add the current approved outcomes, techniques, and safety limits here.

### Back to Head Up Transition

**Prerequisite:** Head Up Side Slides.

**Desired outcome:** Transition safely between a neutral back-flying position
and a neutral head-up position on a defined level without uncontrolled drive or
rotation.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #34.

**Key points:**

- Begin and finish near the center of the tunnel.
- Coordinate the upper- and lower-body rotation instead of throwing one end of
  the body through the transition.
- Arrive with the hips bent and stop in a clearly neutral head-up position.
- Maintain a planned recovery to neutral back flying.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #21, Head Up
Front Flip Transition.

**Coaching and safety:** Begin low and at a controlled wind speed. Require a
smooth, committed transition and a reliable stop before increasing speed or
altitude, and keep the doorway outside the travel path.

**Links:**

- [Back to Sit Transition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U0AOMPKObKQ)

### Neutral Head Up

**Prerequisite:** Completed IBA Back-Flying Level 2.

**Desired outcome:** Fly a symmetrical, neutral, and stable head-up position at
a controlled wind speed without excessive movement or rotation.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #29.

**Key points:**

- Begin on the net with the feet flat, knees bent about 90 degrees, thighs
  parallel to the net, and hips bent about 90 degrees.
- Keep the spine straight, shoulders slightly back, chin up, and arms open to
  the airflow with a small bend at the elbows.
- Maintain a symmetrical shape and use small corrections rather than reaching
  for the net or abruptly standing.
- Practice neutral before adding daffy, knee-flying, or other variations.
- Return to neutral back flying if stability is lost.

**Coaching and safety:** Start with only enough wind to support the vertical
position and increase it only after control is demonstrated. Correct the body
position before requesting more wind, avoid a doorway-facing heading, preserve
the instructor's access path, and keep the back-fly recovery available.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #16.

**Links:**

- [Neutral Sit Position](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ma1_qTxn2QA)

### Head Up Forward & Backward

**Prerequisite:** Neutral and Stable Head Up Position.

**Desired outcome:** Move forward and backward from neutral head-up flight,
stop between movements, and maintain altitude, stability, and control.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #30.

**Key points:**

- Begin low to the net and use small, gradual movements.
- For basic forward drive, keep the lower body neutral and lean the upper body
  slightly back into the airflow; lift the knees to reduce the drive and stop.
- For backward drive, bring the chest slightly toward the airflow and stop with
  the opposing input.
- For stronger movement, coordinate the torso with controlled heel and leg
  inputs while keeping the body balanced.
- Return to neutral after every stop and recover to neutral back flight if the
  position becomes unstable.

**Coaching and safety:** Define the travel path and stopping points before the
flight. Head-up pitch can create rapid drive, so progress from slow starts and
stops, leave generous wall clearance, and increase speed only after control is
demonstrated.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #17.

**Links:**

- [Sit Forward & Backward](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PHxwDgXrCSg)

### Head Up Turns

**Prerequisite:** Neutral and Stable Head Up Position.

**Desired outcome:** Complete controlled turns in both directions and start and
finish accurately on a predetermined heading.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #31.

**Key points:**

- Begin in neutral at the center and select the stopping heading before
  rotating.
- On the net, lean slightly back on the heels; off the net, establish stable
  lift before initiating the turn.
- Shift a lower leg or foot in the direction of rotation, or angle the arms to
  deflect airflow.
- Combine upper- and lower-body inputs only after each can be controlled.
- Use the opposite input to stop and keep the rotation centered.

**Coaching and safety:** Teach one input at a time on or near the net, then
combine inputs only after predictable stops. Watch for forward lean, excessive
pitch, and drift, and keep the bail path and instructor access clear.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #18.

**Links:**

- [Sit Fly Turns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e4M9mC0Tp60)

### Head Up Up & Down

**Prerequisite:** Head Up Left/Right Turns and Forward/Backward Movement.

**Desired outcome:** Move upward, stop, and move downward in the neutral air
column without changing heading or adding forward or backward movement.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #32.

**Key points:**

- Begin low to or on the net in neutral head-up flight.
- To move up, increase lower-body surface area by spreading the feet and
  bringing the knees closer, then add balanced torso and arm input as needed.
- Balance the legs to cancel unwanted drive and return toward neutral to stop.
- To descend, sit more upright, bring the feet and legs closer together, and
  reduce arm surface area.
- Stop the descent with a controlled upward input before reaching the net.

**Coaching and safety:** Set clear upper and lower limits, make small fall-rate
changes, and require a complete stop between directions. Correct pitch before
adding wind or range and preserve the neutral back-flying recovery.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #19.

**Links:**

- [Sit Fly Up & Down](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ps6UULKqoc)

### Head Up Side Slide

**Prerequisite:** Head Up Up and Down Movement.

**Desired outcome:** Shift sideways in both directions with balanced upper- and
lower-body inputs while maintaining heading and altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #33.

**Key points:**

- Start in the center or at one side with the path clear of the doorway.
- Lower and bend the leading arm while extending and raising the trailing arm.
- Lower and angle the leading leg while lifting the trailing leg, balancing the
  upper and lower body to prevent a turn.
- Keep the spine straight and apply the opposing side-slide input early enough
  to stop before the wall.

**Coaching and safety:** Establish the direction, references, and stop limit
before starting. Teach the input on or near the net, increase speed gradually,
and watch for excessive pitch or a late stop.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #20.

**Links:**

- [Sit Side Slides](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--EVE78obVo)

### Head Up Super Positioning

**Description:** Apply head-up control to precise relative positioning around
another flyer.

**Source status:** This skill appears in the supplied progression chart, but the
2024 Static Progression Coach Manual does not provide a standalone lesson plan
for it. Add the current approved positioning standard and safety plan here.

### Head Up Front Flip

**Prerequisite:** Head Up Side Slides.

**Desired outcome:** Complete a smooth head-up-to-head-up front flip on a
defined level without uncontrolled drive, rotation, or altitude change.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #37.

**Key points:**

- Begin and finish in a neutral head-up position near the center.
- Maintain tension from the start of the maneuver without stopping midway.
- Tuck the upper body between the legs while avoiding the knees collapsing into
  the chest.
- Open deliberately and stop in a stable neutral head-up position.

**Manual source:** Static Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #21.

**Coaching and safety:** Begin low and at a controlled wind speed, then increase
the challenge only after smooth completion and reliable stops. The flyer must
continue through a committed transition and use the briefed back-fly recovery
if control is lost.

**Links:**

- [Head Up Front Flip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sx4vvYp7-jg)

## Dynamic

Connect orientations through continuous layouts and low-speed carving lines.
These lessons are summarized from the September 2024 IBA Dynamic Progression
Coach Manual.

### Barrel Roll

**Prerequisite:** Completed all individual Belly-Flying and Back-Flying skills.

**Desired outcome:** Complete controlled half and full barrel-roll transitions
from neutral belly or back flying while preserving heading and altitude.

**Key points:**

- Initiate with the lower body and allow the upper body to follow.
- Keep the legs controlled to limit unwanted forward drive.
- Stop each half in a defined neutral orientation before building the full roll.
- Complete the full roll as one continuous movement only after both halves are
  reliable.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #13.

**Links:**

- [Full Barrel Roll](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzErf8BcMaU)

### Front Layout

**Prerequisite:** Belly-to-Back Transitions Over the Head.

**Desired outcome:** Complete a controlled full belly-to-belly front layout at
a comfortable wind speed while maintaining the intended flight line.

**Key points:**

- Start low to the net in belly flight and initiate upward movement before
  rotation.
- Keep the body long through rotation and use a consistent visual reference.
- Connect the two transition halves without pausing.
- Spot the net and return to a controlled belly orientation.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #16.

**Links:**

- [Front Layout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fqqRkjROlGw)

### Back Layout

**Prerequisite:** Belly-to-Back Transitions Over the Head.

**Desired outcome:** Complete a controlled full back-to-back back layout at a
comfortable wind speed while maintaining the intended flight line.

**Key points:**

- Start low to the net in back flight, feet first toward the planned wall.
- Initiate upward movement before rotation.
- Roll the shoulders forward, extend the legs, and allow the lower body to
  rotate through a long body line.
- Connect both transition halves and spot the net before returning to back
  flight.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #16.

**Links:**

- [Back Layout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S98wSLVQ9dM)

### Head Down Out Face (LS)

**Prerequisite:** Back-Fly Level 2.

**Desired outcome:** Carve the tunnel circumference in a controlled low-speed
head-down out-face orientation while remaining close to the center.

**Key points:**

- Initiate with a side slide and add forward drive to continue the rotation.
- Keep the head near the center as the body rotates around it.
- Use consistent visual references and increase angle and speed gradually.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #17.

**Links:**

- [Head Down Outface Low Speed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=INqeEdWkn8g)

### Head Up Out Face (LS)

**Prerequisite:** Back-Fly Level 2.

**Desired outcome:** Maintain a controlled low-speed, head-up out-face carve
with consistent radius, altitude, proximity, and awareness.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #71.

**Key points:**

- Initiate with a side slide and add backward drive to continue the rotation.
- Keep the knees and feet near the center while the upper body rotates around
  them.
- Balance the inputs continuously to control speed, radius, and altitude.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #18.

**Links:**

- [Head Up Outface Low Speed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5sI6vrEQ0bU)

### Head Down In Face (LS)

**Prerequisite:** Back-Fly Level 2.

**Desired outcome:** Carve the tunnel circumference in a controlled low-speed
head-down in-face orientation while remaining close to the center.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #61.

**Key points:**

- Initiate with a side slide and add forward drive to continue the rotation.
- Keep the head near the center as the body rotates around it.
- Use wall and center references to maintain the intended path.

**Manual source:** Dynamic Progression Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #19.

**Links:**

- [Head Down In-face Low Speed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ggCq9jY5og)

### Head Up In Face (LS)

**Prerequisite:** Back-Fly Level 2.

**Desired outcome:** Maintain a controlled low-speed, head-up in-face carve with
consistent radius, altitude, proximity, and awareness.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #66.

**Key points:**

- Initiate with a side slide and add feet-first drive toward the center.
- Keep the knees and feet near the center while the upper body rotates around

- Balance the inputs continuously to control speed, radius, and altitude.

## Formations

Add controlled entrances and shared formations after the required individual
flight skills are established.

### 2-Way Entrances

**Prerequisite:** Super Positioning.

**Desired outcome:** Enter as a linked two-flyer group and establish the
briefed formation at a controlled, low altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorials #8, #9, and #10.

**Manual scope:** The Formation manual includes linked group entrances in its
Level 2 two-way lesson. The progression chart displays entrances in Level 3,
so this page keeps that chart placement.

**Key points:**

- Brief the entrance order, grips, slots, level, and release plan before the
  flight.
- Both flyers are responsible for stopping the entrance and remaining low in
  the tunnel.
- Maintain visual contact and establish a stable formation before beginning
  another movement.
- If a flyer becomes unstable, that flyer waits for the instructor while the
  stable flyer moves low and away.

**Coaching and safety:** Coordinate the entire entrance with the on-duty
instructor. Use only a formation and entry method appropriate to both flyers'
demonstrated skill.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #8.

### 3-Way Formations

**Prerequisite:** 2-Way Flying.

**Desired outcome:** Fly with two other people while maintaining control and
visual awareness, then complete predetermined formations and selections from
the three-way dive pool.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #12.

**Key points:**

- Begin with basic formations, small movements, and easy transitions.
- Maintain visual contact and stop in a neutral body position before starting
  the next movement.
- Coordinate vertical exercises with clear timing and visual references.
- Enter into assigned slots and increase complexity only after the group can
  maintain level, heading, and control.

**Coaching and safety:** Use three qualified flyers or an appropriate
flyer-and-coach combination. Brief slots, entrances, transitions, recovery,
and separation; if one flyer becomes unstable, that flyer waits for the
instructor while stable flyers move low and away.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #9.
