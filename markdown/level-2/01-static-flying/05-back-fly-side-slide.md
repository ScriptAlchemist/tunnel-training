### Back Fly Side Slide

**Prerequisite:** Back-fly up and down movement.

**Desired outcome:** Shift sideways in both directions using balanced
whole-body inputs, then stop on heading without gaining or losing altitude.

**Key points:**

- Begin in neutral at the center or one side of the tunnel.
- Combine upper- and lower-body inputs to deflect airflow evenly to one side.
- Adjust the balance of each input to maintain heading and apply the opposite
  input to stop.
- For faster slides, add torso and hip pitch and begin the stopping input
  earlier.

**Coaching and safety:** Establish the direction, travel limit, and stop point
before starting. Increase speed only after accurate heading and altitude
control are consistent.
