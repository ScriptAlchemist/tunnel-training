### Back Fly Enter & Exit

**Prerequisite:** Back-fly up and down movement.

**Desired outcome:** Enter into a neutral back-flying position and approach
and exit the doorway safely, in control, at the correct altitude, and with
minimal instructor assistance.

**Key points:**

- Begin in the doorway with the back toward the airflow and hands on the frame.
- Ease back with the chin up and torso flat, then release and extend the arms to
  manage the descent.
- Push from the floor and use balanced inputs to reach the center.
- For the exit, point the feet toward the doorway from about three feet above
  the net, move backward slowly, place the feet on the floor, and use the upper
  body to rotate upright.

**Coaching and safety:** Maintain heading with small lower-body corrections and
coordinate the entire exercise with the on-duty instructor. The student enters
only after receiving the instructor's signal.
