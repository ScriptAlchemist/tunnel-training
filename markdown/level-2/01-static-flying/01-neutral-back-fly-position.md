### Neutral Back Fly Position

**Prerequisite:** Completed IBA Level 1 Flyer Skills.

**Desired outcome:** Maintain full control in a neutral back-flying position at
a neutral wind speed, using heading and forward/backward corrections as needed
to remain positioned in the tunnel.

**Key points:**

- Keep the spine straight and torso flat to the airflow.
- Bend the hips so the thighs are approximately 90 degrees to the torso, then
  bend the knees approximately 90 degrees.
- Keep the knees shoulder-width apart, press the arms back into the airflow,
  and keep the chin up.
- Use small, symmetrical corrections to hold heading and position.

**Coaching and safety:** It is normal to begin at a wind speed that keeps the
student on the net until they can control heading and forward/backward
movement. Increase speed and altitude only with demonstrated stability.
