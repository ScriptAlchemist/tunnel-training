### Back Forward & Backward

**Prerequisite:** Neutral and stable back-flying position.

**Desired outcome:** Move forward and backward with correct upper- and
lower-body inputs, stop between movements, and maintain altitude, stability,
heading, and control.

**Key points:**

- Extend and retract the arms or legs to start and stop basic movements.
- Maintain a symmetrical shape and manage heading with small upper-body
  adjustments.
- Stop and return to neutral before starting a movement in the other direction.
- Combine upper- and lower-body inputs for smoother or faster advanced
  movement, then account for the additional stopping distance.

**Coaching and safety:** Inputs made on the net have a stronger effect than the
same inputs off the net. Use clear reference points and leave enough distance
for a controlled stop.
