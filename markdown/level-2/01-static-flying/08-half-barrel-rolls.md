### Half Barrel Rolls

**Prerequisite:** Completed all individual belly- and back-flying skills.

**Manual scope:** The lesson covers both half and full barrel-roll transitions.

**Desired outcome:** Transition between neutral belly and back positions while
holding the original heading and altitude and avoiding unwanted drive toward
the wall.

**Key points:**

- Initiate the roll with the lower body and knees before allowing the upper
  body to follow.
- Extend the arms slightly forward to keep the upper body from dropping.
- Keep the legs bent or neutral to limit forward, head-first drive.
- Stop the rotation on heading in a defined neutral belly or back position.

**Coaching and safety:** Perform early transitions low over the net. The coach
should remain on their feet, leave the spotting path open, and watch closely
for head-low pitch and forward drive.
