### Over the Feet Transitions

**Prerequisite:** Completed all individual belly- and back-flying skills.

**Desired outcome:** Transition over the feet from a neutral belly or back
position to the opposite neutral position without excessive wall drive,
heading change, or altitude change.

**Key points:**

- Begin around waist height above the net and increase upper-body surface area
  to create lift.
- For belly-to-back, drive the knees toward the chest as rotation begins.
- For back-to-belly, keep the knees bent and drive the feet under the body.
- Prepare the stopping input before the target orientation and return to
  neutral only after rotation has stopped.

**Coaching and safety:** Early attempts require active instructor spotting.
Watch for straight legs or a continuing torso arch that causes over-rotation,
and avoid pausing vertically because that can produce a rapid descent.
