### Back Fly Turns

**Prerequisite:** Neutral and stable back-flying position.

**Desired outcome:** Complete controlled turns in both directions using
balanced upper- and lower-body inputs, beginning and ending accurately on a
predetermined heading.

**Key points:**

- Begin and finish in the neutral back-flying position.
- Direct airflow opposite the intended turn using the arms or lower body.
- Keep the torso flat and use the opposite input to stop.
- For advanced turns, coordinate upper and lower body to rotate around the
  center point; larger inputs create faster rotation.

**Coaching and safety:** Provide a clear heading reference, teach controlled
stops before increasing turn rate, and account for the stronger effect of
inputs while the student is on the net.
