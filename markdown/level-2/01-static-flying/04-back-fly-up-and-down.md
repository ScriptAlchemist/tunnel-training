### Back Fly Up & Down

**Prerequisite:** Back-fly turns plus forward and backward movement.

**Desired outcome:** Move up, stop, and move down in the air column without
moving forward, backward, or changing heading.

**Key points:**

- To move up, slightly arch the torso and widen or extend the arms and legs to
  increase surface area.
- Return to neutral to stop upward movement.
- To move down, slightly de-arch the torso and reduce arm and leg extension.
- Balance all inputs to avoid unwanted drive and use the arms and lower legs to
  maintain heading.

**Coaching and safety:** Avoid large changes or curling into a tight ball.
Begin with small fall-rate adjustments and keep the student's altitude within
their demonstrated ability.
