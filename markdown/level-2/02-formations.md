## Formations

Apply individual flying skills to shared levels, approaches, positioning, and
safe movement with other flyers.

### 2-Way Formations

**Prerequisite:** Super Positioning.

**Desired outcome:** Fly with another person while maintaining control and
visual awareness, then build predetermined two-way formations with deliberate
approaches and stops.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorials #8, #9, and #10.

**Key points:**

- Begin with one flyer holding position while the other moves, then switch
  roles.
- Maintain visual contact and make small, controlled movements before adding
  more complex transitions.
- Stop in a neutral body position before beginning the next movement.
- Build formations on level and arrive in the correct relative position before
  taking grips.

**Coaching and safety:** Use a coach, instructor, or competent Level 1 flyer as
the second participant. Brief the formation, individual slots, entrance,
recovery, and separation plan before flying.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #8.

### 2-Way Verticals

**Prerequisite:** Super Positioning.

**Desired outcome:** Complete coordinated vertical exercises with another
flyer while maintaining visual contact, heading, relative level, and control.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorials #8, #9, and #10.

**Key points:**

- Establish the starting formation and visual references before changing
  levels.
- Coordinate the timing of each vertical movement with the other flyer.
- Use the whole body to control fall rate while preserving the planned
  horizontal position.
- Stop in a neutral body position before beginning the next movement.

**Coaching and safety:** Begin with small changes in level and increase the
range only after both flyers demonstrate coordinated stops. Brief each flyer's
slot, altitude limits, recovery, and separation plan.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #8.

**Resources:**

- [IBA 2-Way Verticals](https://www.tunnelflight.com/skills/flyer-formation-level-2/2-way-verticals/)

### Belly Super Positioning

**Prerequisite:** Belly-Flying Up & Down.

**Desired outcome:** Combine upper- and lower-body controls to move precisely
around another flyer while maintaining stability, heading, and altitude.

**Reference material:** IBA Flight Tutorial #11.

**Key points:**

- Learn lateral movement first, then add rotation while maintaining eye
  contact and altitude.
- Use clear visual references to stop in the intended relative position.
- Combine lateral and vertical movement only after each can be controlled
  independently.
- Progress to coordinated lateral, vertical, and rotational movement using the
  whole body.

**Coaching and safety:** Start with one movement axis at a time and use small,
controlled inputs. Increase complexity only when the flyer can stop accurately
and remain aware of the other participant.

**Manual source:** Formation Skydiving Coach Manual, Lesson Plan #7.
